Put single-faced official MTG card images here, named exactly after the real card.

Examples:
  Sol Ring.png
  Angel of Vitality.jpg

A file placed directly in this folder is assigned to Alex.
You can instead use the alex, will, miguel, or jay subfolder to assign its owner.

After you upload and commit the image to GitHub, the importer runs automatically.
Successful images are removed from incoming only after the published image, catalog,
Cockatrice XML, and manifest all validate.
